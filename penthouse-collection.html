Four faces along a corridor set the rhythm for a collection of eight paintings. Across the rooms, the gaze shifts from a white owl to an imagined creature, a portrait in saturated color, and a Berlin street.

The eight works

Patch built the collection around Casey Brookbush’s four square paintings from 2020. Brookbush confirms that the four belong together. Seen in sequence, a familiar face shifts by degrees until the final image looks straight back at the viewer.

Casey Brookbush · Ascension Series

Beyond the series

Brookbush carries the figure into a night sky in NIGHTOWL IV. Ward’s Quadrupus lets the color fall away entirely. Shapiro brings it back in a small, intensely colored portrait. With Berlin, Nunn draws the eye past the picture plane and down a street.

In the residence

The corridor study groups the four squares on one wall. These four mock-ups give each remaining work a room of its own and show its scale beside a stairway, a bed, a sofa, or a console.

Private sale · Price on request

Acquire the collection

Nigel Patch offers all eight works together in a single private sale. Collectors, art advisors, and residential designers can request pricing and the full presentation dossier directly from him.

The dossier records each work’s dimensions and ownership history and includes provenance confirmations from all four artists. Ward identifies Quadrupus as the headlining work of his Power of Grayscale showcase, where Patch acquired it.
