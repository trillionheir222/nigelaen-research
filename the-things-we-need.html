A science-fiction novel by Nigel Patch

The Things
We Need

Sam and Clara Morrow own two umbrellas. The sky is clear. Their new delivery service thinks they need a third.

Before they place the order

Sam first imagines the service as a joke while he and Clara are looking for the remote. Someday, he figures, the delivery van will set out before anyone has ordered a thing. Then an invitation to a paid household trial lands in his inbox. Clara is pregnant, the clinic bills are real, and six months of letting a retailer study their routines would buy them some breathing room.

When the umbrella arrives, the rain starts while Clara is looking for her keys. Sam logs a successful prediction. Clara takes the umbrella to work. From the delivery service’s point of view, that settles the matter. From inside the marriage, there are two people who might tell the story differently.

As the packages grow more personal, the question changes. How do Sam and Clara work out what they want once an answer is already sitting on their kitchen counter?
