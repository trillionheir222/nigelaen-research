Morphæmatics

A mathematical language for meaning,
relationships, and transformation.

Morphæmatics is a developing formal framework that asks how meaning and context can be expressed mathematically. It explores ways to describe how things relate, how structures change, and what remains recognizable through those changes.

The ideaResearch questionsWork developedCurrent development

The idea at a glance

How we express a problem shapes what we can see in it.

Giving relationships
a mathematical form.

A mathematical language gives us objects to reason about, rules for working with them, and ways to state precise claims. Morphæmatics develops these tools around questions of meaning, organization, and context.

Its ambition is to make relationships easier to express, compare, and investigate, including relationships that can be awkward to describe in everyday language.

Imagine Alex teaches Sam mathematics, while Sam teaches Alex music. The people stay the same, but their roles reverse with the subject. Calling Alex simply “the teacher” loses part of that relationship.

Morphæmatics aims to give such distinctions a precise mathematical expression, so identity, relationship, and context can be considered together. The intended benefit is being able to reason explicitly about what changes and what stays the same as roles and contexts shift. This is a simple illustration of the research goal.

Areas of inquiry

What does the language
need to express?

The framework brings several connected questions into a common mathematical setting. These questions give a public overview of the territory being explored.

Identity & representation

What makes something count as the same object when its description or surrounding context changes?

Relations & context

How can the role of an object depend on its relationships, and how can that dependence be made explicit?

Structure & composition

How do parts combine into a whole, and how does their organization affect what can be said about that whole?

Transformation & preservation

When a structure changes, which features change with it, and which relationships persist?

Notation & expression

How can symbols and rules make these distinctions precise enough to work with, compare, and communicate?

Formal claims & examples

What follows from the definitions? Which claims can be justified, and where do examples expose limits or open questions?

Work already developed

Four substantial manuscript drafts form the current body of work.

A framework
taking written form.

The manuscript corpus develops mathematical objects, relationships, operations, and rules for forming expressions. It also contains formal claims, proof arguments, and worked examples. This provides a substantial foundation for continued refinement.

  • Formal foundationsDefinitions, constructions, and rules establish the objects and relationships the framework works with.
  • Mathematical developmentPropositions, conjectures, proof arguments, and examples explore what follows from those foundations.
  • Symbolic languageNotation is being developed to express the framework's distinctions and support further work.

The question ahead

What can this language help us understand—and how clearly can that be demonstrated?

Development
continues.

The present work includes refining the formal system, developing its notation, and examining the claims made within the manuscripts. Its broader mathematical value is an open research question.

Active developmentFormal coherence, clarity of expression, and demonstrations of use guide the next stage.

The manuscripts and technical details are currently private. Future public material may introduce selected definitions and examples as they become ready for release.
