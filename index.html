Orientation

This archive documents a research practice at the intersection of relational mathematics, future-facing intelligence systems, novel compute, and technological design.

Entries distinguish public work, implemented and tested systems, active research, conceptual work, and plans. The archive does not disclose proprietary implementations, private invention records, confidential partner information, or claims awaiting validation.
