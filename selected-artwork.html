Nigel Patch · Visual art

Selected
Artwork

A body becomes the spoke of a wheel. Hands close around a globe or meet at the center of a necklace. These drawings, paintings, and objects trace the forms that recur in Patch’s visual work.

Bodies in formation Geometry and motion Hands, eyes and emblems Portraits and imagined scenes

Bodies in formation

Look for the spaces between the limbs. In these drawings, those gaps often determine the shape of the whole group.

Geometry and motion

A spiral can read as a surface, a path, or a measure of time. Follow a single line to see where the reading changes.

Hands, eyes and emblems

The hand recurs at several scales, from a pendant on a chain to a pair of palms large enough to hold a city.

Portraits and imagined scenes

Some works unfold like scenes. Others build a face a plane, a strip, or a color at a time.
