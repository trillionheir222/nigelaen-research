Biography

A self-directed path through research, enterprise, and art.

Nigel Patch (Nigelæn) was born and raised in Seattle, Washington. After leaving conventional high school to study online, he chose the GED path and entered college a year earlier than his original school cohort.

One quarter of college made the next decision clear: the pace and scope of formal study did not match the questions he wanted to pursue. He left to build a self-directed education, spending roughly the next twelve years auditing online courses, working through textbooks across disciplines, and building a practical, continually renewed personal curriculum.

That learning path developed alongside work in enterprise and the arts. Patch founded Prismatii, his first successful company, and has shown and sold artwork through a gallery in West Seattle. Those experiences remain part of the portfolio’s wider logic: research is not separated from making, visual thinking, or the realities of bringing work into the world.

Today, the public record crosses mathematical notation, theoretical physics, computational architecture, visual research instruments, invention design, and speculative creative practice. The common method is to build the languages, instruments, and records a question requires.

This portfolio distinguishes public work, bounded implementation, active research, conceptual work, and material held back for privacy, intellectual-property, or publication reasons.

How to read this archive

Ambition needs a visible boundary.

Some pages describe published books or an implemented, bounded reference system. Others present developing frameworks, research directions, or public-safe category maps. They should not be read as commercial products, established scientific consensus, or evidence for confidential work.

Protected work is not an omission or a teaser. It is a deliberate choice to avoid premature disclosure, unsupported claims, and avoidable compromise of future publication or intellectual-property decisions.
