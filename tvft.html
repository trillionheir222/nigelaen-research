TVFT
Time-Void
Field Theory

A fourteen-volume attempt to ask whether temporal structure can supply a unified language for problems spanning quantum theory, gravity, matter, collapse, and cosmology.

What this is

A source-to-observable theoretical architecture.

TVFT is Nigel Patch’s independently authored theoretical-physics program. It begins from a proposed temporal source structure, introduces directed temporal fields, induced phase and reduction language, then follows the resulting architecture toward collapse-compatible observable dynamics and matter-bearing structure.

The later volumes then ask whether this proposed architecture can be put into matrix and operator form, recover familiar structures in controlled regimes, and generate disciplined diagnostic or test-facing questions. “Theory of everything” describes the program’s target, not an established result.

Plain-English orientation

A reader’s aid, not the formal definition.

For readers new to the vocabulary: this section translates the program’s terms into ordinary language. It simplifies the formal account for orientation only; the published books define the theory’s objects, assumptions, and claims precisely.

STARTING LAYER

Temporal source structure

The framework’s proposed deepest bookkeeping layer: a way to describe temporal organization before starting with the ordinary visible world. It is a theoretical starting point, not an observed object.

MATHEMATICAL LANGUAGE

Directed temporal fields

A proposed way of writing down how temporal relations, directions, and changes are organized. “Field” here means a mathematical description spread across a system, not a confirmed new physical field.

GETTING TO THE FAMILIAR

Reduction & realization

“Reduction” asks how a deeper description could appear as a familiar one at the scale we observe. “Matter realization” is TVFT’s proposed account of how stable, observable material structure might emerge.

HOW IT EARNS SCRUTINY

Operators, recovery & tests

Matrices and operators are tools for making the proposal calculable. “Recovery” means checking whether known physics appears in a defined regime; it does not mean that known physics has been replaced. A diagnostic pathway names what later data would need to decide.

The portfolio record is not asking visitors to accept TVFT. It is asking them to see the scale, structure, and testability ambitions of the written program.

Quick read: Book I develops the source, reduction, collapse/matter, and operator arcs. Book II tests the architecture against familiar physics, then extends it toward geometry, boundary, information, diagnostic, and cosmological questions.

Claim-status discipline

Not every statement in the program carries the same weight.

Book II explicitly separates the following kinds of statements. That distinction is part of the research method: a formal definition is not a physical observation, and a conditional derivation is not an experimental confirmation.

01

Accepted-physics recovery

Shows how an established mathematical structure may be recovered in a stated regime.

02

TVFT definition

Introduces a proposed object, map, variable, or relation internal to the framework.

03

Conditional derivation

Follows under the assumptions and controlled conditions written in the relevant section.

04

Interpretive bridge

Offers a proposed reading that relates the framework to an established physical concept.

05

Diagnostic pathway

Specifies what a future comparison, simulation, observation, or measurement would need to evaluate.

How the program is organized

From formal proposal to physical question.

TVFT works across formal and interpretive layers rather than presenting one isolated hypothesis. The publications move between mathematical objects, proposed relationships, and questions about whether a framework can recover or constrain familiar physical descriptions.

This is why the program is best read as a connected research architecture, not as a short claim that can be judged from a single slogan.

Recurring terrain

  • Time, proper time, and temporal structure
  • Field, phase, and potential formulations
  • Operators, matrices, and collapse dynamics
  • Quantum correlation, information, and geometry
  • Boundary, horizon, black-hole, and cosmological questions

Research-status boundary

Ambitious theory is not settled physics.

  • a peer-reviewed consensus theory of gravity, quantum mechanics, matter, or cosmology
  • experimental validation, independent replication, or accepted replacement of established physical theories
  • that mathematical formalization alone demonstrates physical truth
  • that the project’s explanatory ambition has already been achieved
